# Tirumala IIT & Medical Academy
## Non-Technical Staff User Guide: How to Update Your Website

Welcome to your new website management console. This guide is written for non-technical school staff. You do **not** need any coding knowledge or a web developer to update student results, post notices, upload model papers, or add gallery photos.

---

### 1. Logging Into Your Staff Portal
1. Open your web browser and go to: `https://yourdomain.com/admin` (or `http://localhost:8000/admin`).
2. Enter your credentials:
   - **Username**: `admin`
   - **Password**: `tirumala@2026` *(You can change this password at any time in MySQL)*.
3. Click **Sign In to Dashboard**.

---

### 2. How to Add a New Student Result / Rank
*Whenever new JEE Advanced, NEET, JEE Main, or Inter board examination results are declared:*
1. In the top admin menu, click on **Results** (or click the green **"+ Add New Result"** button on the Dashboard).
2. Fill out the simple form:
   - **Student Full Name**: (e.g. `K. Sai Teja`)
   - **Roll Number**: (e.g. `TIMA202401`)
   - **Stream**: Select `MPC`, `BiPC`, or `Foundation`
   - **Exam Type**: Select `JEE Advanced`, `NEET`, `JEE Main`, `IPE Inter`, or `SSC Class 10`
   - **Year**: (e.g. `2025` or `2026`)
   - **Campus**: Select one of the 5 campuses (`Rajamahendravaram`, `Visakhapatnam`, `Bhimavaram`, `Tanuku`, `Payakaraopeta`)
   - **Score / Rank Achieved**: (e.g. `AIR 142 (Top in AP)` or `Score: 695/720`)
   - **Photo / Certificate (Optional)**: Choose an image from your computer
   - **Feature on Homepage**: Check this box if you want this ranker to display prominently on the homepage banner.
3. Click **Save & Publish Result**. The result goes live immediately!

---

### 3. How to Post an Urgent Notice or Announcement Banner
*Use this for admission deadlines, holiday notices, scholarship test dates, or exam alerts:*
1. Click on **Notices** in the top menu.
2. Click **"+ Post New Notice"**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Headline**: (e.g. `Admissions Open for Academic Year 2025-26`)
   - **Badge Tag**: Choose `Urgent` (Red), `Admission` (Amber), `New`, or `Exam`
   - **Clickable Link**: Type `/admissions.php` or paste any web link
   - **Display Active on Website**: Keep this checked to show it in the top red banner.
4. Click **Save Notice**. It will immediately appear across all pages of the website.

---

### 4. How to Upload a Model Question Paper (PDF)
*To publish free practice papers for students across Andhra Pradesh:*
1. Click on **Model Papers** in the top menu.
2. Click **"+ Upload New Paper"**.
3. Enter:
   - **Paper Title**: (e.g. `Into 10th Class Entrance Screening Model Paper 2025`)
   - **Grade / Class**: Select `Class 6`, `Class 7`, `Class 8`, `Class 9`, `Class 10`, or `Intermediate`
   - **Board**: Select `CBSE`, `ICSE`, or `AP State Board`
   - **Upload PDF File**: Click "Choose File" and select the PDF document from your computer.
4. Click **Save & Publish Model Paper**. Students can now download it directly from the Model Papers page.

---

### 5. How to Add Photos to the Gallery
*For Annual Day photos, Sports Meet pictures, or Rank Felicitation ceremonies:*
1. Click on **Gallery** in the top menu.
2. Click **"+ Add Gallery Photo"**.
3. Choose the **Category**: `Events & Annual Day`, `Results & Felicitations`, or `Sports & Games`.
4. Choose the image file from your computer.
5. Add a short caption and click **Add to Gallery**.

---

### 6. How to Manage Admissions Enquiries
*When parents or students submit the online admission form:*
1. Click on **Enquiries** in the top menu.
2. You will see their name, parent name, phone number, preferred campus, course, and whether they paid the ₹500 application fee.
3. Click **"Mark Contacted"** after you call the parent so your team knows the lead is being handled.
4. Click **"Export to CSV"** anytime to download an Excel-compatible spreadsheet of all student applicants.

---
*For technical hosting questions or domain changes, contact your IT administrator.*
